# Manual Fact List — our test set

**Fill this in during Step 0, before writing any extraction code.**

Open the three PDFs and read about 15 pages. Every time you see a clear fact,
write it down here with its page number. Aim for 15 to 20.

**Why this matters.** There is no answer key for this task. Without one, "does the
system work?" has no answer better than a shrug. This list *is* the answer key.
At Step 11 we check how many of these the system found, and that gives us a real
number to report: *"the system recovered 16 of the 20 facts identified by hand;
here are the 4 it missed and why."*

That sentence is worth more in an interview than any amount of polish.

**Pick a mix.** Some easy ones from ordinary sentences. Some hard ones from
tables. At least one where the same thing appears in two different documents. At
least one where two numbers look contradictory.
